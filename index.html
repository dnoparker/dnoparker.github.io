How to use

Step one

Find a spot with good natural lighting and hold your camera steady. Keep your head centered in the frame and make sure your face is clearly visible.

Step two

Keep your face inside the circle until it turns green, then hold still while the system analyzes your skin tone to find your perfect shade match.

The AI will analyze your skin tone and suggest a shade match, but you'll have the freedom to select whichever tone feels most authentic to you.
